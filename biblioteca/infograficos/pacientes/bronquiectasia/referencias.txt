BRONQUIECTASIA - REFERENCIAS
Atualizacao: 27 de setembro de 2026

Material educativo para pacientes. Nao substitui a consulta nem a orientacao
da equipe de saude.

1. Pereira MC, Athanazio RA, Dalcin PTR, et al.
   Consenso brasileiro sobre bronquiectasias nao fibrocisticas.
   J Bras Pneumol. 2019;45(4):e20190122.
   DOI: 10.1590/1806-3713/e20190122 | PMID: 31411280 | PMCID: PMC6733718

2. Thomson R, Thornton C, Aksamit T, et al.
   Management of Adult Bronchiectasis: An American College of Chest
   Physicians Clinical Practice Guideline.
   CHEST. 2026 (publicado online em 21/07/2026).
   DOI: 10.1016/j.chest.2026.06.057 | PMID: 42480819
   (Mesma referencia usada no Agente Bronquiectasias 2026 do PneumoAgents.)

3. Chalmers JD, Haworth CS, Flume PA, et al.
   European Respiratory Society clinical practice guideline for the
   management of adult bronchiectasis.
   Eur Respir J. 2025;66(6):2501126.
   DOI: 10.1183/13993003.01126-2025 | PMID: 41016738

4. Swenson C, Schraufnagel D, Sadikot R, et al.
   What is Bronchiectasis? ATS Patient Education Series.
   Am J Respir Crit Care Med. 2017;195(8):P15-P16.
   DOI: 10.1164/rccm.1958P15 | PMID: 28409684
   (Definicao; causas: infeccoes previas, imunidade, doencas geneticas,
   aspiracao; cerca de 40% sem causa identificada; nao reversivel, mas
   tratavel; sintomas e exacerbacao.)

5. Swenson C, Schraufnagel D, Sadikot R, et al.
   Treating Bronchiectasis. ATS Patient Education Series.
   Am J Respir Crit Care Med. 2017;196(10):P19-P20.
   DOI: 10.1164/rccm.19610P19 | PMID: 29140121
   (Limpeza das vias aereas diaria, exercicio regular, parar de fumar e
   evitar fumaca, vacinacao, reabilitacao pulmonar.)
